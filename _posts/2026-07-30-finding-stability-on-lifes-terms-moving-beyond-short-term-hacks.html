Finding Stability on Life's Terms: Moving Beyond Short-Term Hacks

A Living Mindfully Interactive Session

It’s easy to feel balanced when things are going smoothly. But how do we work with our minds when life doesn't go our way?

If we treat mindfulness as a quick fix to secure temporary happiness or escape daily friction, our comfort zone inevitably shrinks. True spiritual growth requires patient endurance, a willingness to expand our capacity for life’s challenges, and a commitment to long-term inner freedom.

Join us for our upcoming 90-minute online gathering as we quiet the busy mind, explore deep teachings, and share reflections on navigating lay life with wisdom and grace.

Gathering Schedule:

  • Opening Reflection (10 mins): Grounding ourselves in the core purpose of mindfulness—moving past temporary "hacks" to find genuine, long-term peace.

  • Guided Meditation (20 mins): Somatic body awareness to calm a fast-paced mind and settle the nervous system.

  • Dharma Talk (30 mins): Featuring the scriptural tale of the dog-duty and ox-duty ascetics. We will discuss:

    • The danger of concentration without discernment.

    • Embracing the wisdom of "not sure" in times of uncertainty.

    • Training our attention to align with long-term aspirations rather than fleeting relief.

  • Group Discussion & Q&A (30 mins): An open, non-judgmental container to connect and share real-world practices.
